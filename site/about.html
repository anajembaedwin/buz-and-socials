About us

Who we are and what we do

Buz and Socials Ventures is based in Abuja. It brings together Reservations, Staffing, Technologies, Agro and Consulting.

No matter which one you need, you call the same number and write to the same email, and a person answers.

Our founder

Edwin Anajemba

Founder, Buz and Socials Ventures

Edwin started Buz and Socials with hotel bookings in Abuja and has grown it into a group of businesses covering reservations, staffing, software, agro-trading and consulting.

He stands behind every business in the group. When you call or write, you reach a person, and the job is done the way it was promised.
